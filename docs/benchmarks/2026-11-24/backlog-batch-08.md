The strongest unresolved candidate is four relay workers at the same four-vCPU budget. Three earlier one-port host pairs gained 49.2–80.8% at a fixed 128-event callback budget, with a median paired gain of 63.9%. The independent four-vCPU VM holdout on the older build had a median Micropod/OrbStack throughput ratio of 0.854 (95% interval 0.709–1.259, 15 common clean pairs from 16 attempts). Closing the paired point estimate would take about 17.1% more receiver throughput. The Micropod side met the 75 ms fresh-p95 limit in all 16 attempts. The host result does not establish how much gain transfers to the VM. [Original host and VM comparisons](../2026-11-22/gamma-tcp-contrasts.md)

The revised direct service already requests fq on its private publication interface when it encounters the built-in noqueue. An archived direct service transmitted 269.17 GB on `mppub0` and 420 bytes on `eth0` between the two scope snapshots. The archived readbacks omit the active root queue. The older +17.7% service-fq result came from replacing noqueue on a bridge-path service. The new read-only capture can establish whether fq already handles bytes on the actual direct interface. At 14:24 AEDT the read-only Cuttlefish project showed fresh leases of 5/6 on Gamma-128GB.local and 1/1 on isolated Linux. Neither project handoff contained a current owner release or a qualified revised baseline. The next timed test is the stock four-vCPU, two-worker baseline, then two versus four workers with one listener and 128 events. These need the released serving scopes, idle eligible runners, effective disk clearance throughout and fixture preflight. The eBPF diagnostic and independent randomized matched peer holdout retain their own admission and scoring. No fresh VM or peer timing ran in this batch.

An additional replay of an existing six-pair VM trial showed why a lower event count can be misleading. Widening service pacing timer slack reduced scheduling deferrals per GiB in 6/6 pairs, yet throughput improved in only 2/6, and the 75 ms fresh-p95 guardrail passed in 3/6 instead of 4/6. This supplemental diagnostic and the direct-path correction each add zero to the batch count.
