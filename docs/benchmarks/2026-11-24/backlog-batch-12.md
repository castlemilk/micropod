An independent relay experiment tested whether a short bulk read could yield its unused 256 KiB allowance to another ready stream in the same poll. With mixed 128/384 KiB transfers, candidate throughput changed +5.52% across eight pairs, but short-reply p95 rose from 0.201 to 0.274 ms and worsened in every pair. The data support reducing call counts in mixed bursts, while leaving the size of the throughput benefit uncertain. Keep the 256 KiB read cap.
