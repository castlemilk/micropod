**24 November 2026 · 230.08 · Coalescing accepted-descriptor handoffs on a serial worker**

Local/host-only Darwin 25.5 arm64. The control ran Target.adopt extracted from Micropod a60d203b16b24abd857e1b5c9e817636b241f850. The candidate queued accepted descriptors in a locked FIFO and submitted one dispatch block when the FIFO changed from empty to nonempty. When that block ran it detached and adopted the whole FIFO. Both used real serial DispatchQueues. A marker-and-EOF socketpair handler stood in for the backend dial. The experiment measured submission count and service order, with two or four workers and 32 handoffs in fixed rotation.

An intentionally stalled worker set received all 32 handoffs. After 0, 1, 16, 31 or 32 submissions, each worker also received a callback for an existing readable socket. For the 32-handoff burst, dispatch submissions fell from 32 to two with two workers, or four with four. At the midpoint, 16 control handoffs were processed before their respective workers' ready callbacks. Coalescing raised that to all 32. With readiness inserted after only one handoff, the counts rose from one to 16 on two workers, or one to eight on four. Readiness inserted first stayed first. When each handoff was allowed to finish before the next admission, both arms needed 32 submissions.

Twelve fixed cases repeated twice per arm in ABBA order. Both repetitions agreed in all cells. Each arm delivered 768/768 handoff echoes and 72/72 replies on existing sockets, preserved per-owner FIFO, and checked EOF on both ends. These deterministic positions have no sampling interval. No wire loss, fresh p95/p99, CPU/GiB, runnable time or elapsed throughput was measured. The bounded fixture isolated a blocked or fully drained owner and did not model overlapping callback execution, lease release or all scheduling states.

Keep per-descriptor dispatch in the worker baseline. Coalescing is worth a real timing trial only if accept bursts accumulate on already busy owners, and a batch must avoid starving a ready callback queued between accepts.
