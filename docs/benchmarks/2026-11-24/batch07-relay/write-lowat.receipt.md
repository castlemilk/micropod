**24 November 2026 · 230.03 · Requesting a writable threshold for pending relay output**

Local/host-only Darwin 25.5.0 arm64, Apple clang 21.0.0. At Micropod `a60d203b16b24abd857e1b5c9e817636b241f850`, the stock relay arms an ordinary writable filter when it saves the unsent portion of a write. The only candidate change requested NOTE_LOWAT on that EVFILT_WRITE registration at the smaller of 32 KiB and the pending suffix. The source, read cap, owner and callback budget were fixed within each comparison. This tested output readiness, separately from prior read watermark experiments.

The production relay and Darwin kqueue forwarded 4 or 32 KiB over pressured anonymous stream sockets. The sink released 2, 8 or 32 KiB at a time. Six fixed cases ran twice per arm in ABBA order. All 12 paired cases agreed on the first progress point, full-flush point and number of flush writes. A 32 KiB suffix needed 16, four or one flush writes for the respective room steps under both settings. Both arms first advanced on the first step. Per arm all 221,184 payload bytes, 12 reverse markers, both FIN directions and all retirements verified.

A companion private IPv4 TCP fixture registered the same filter after the sender encountered EAGAIN. It compared each tested filter with a separate default filter on the same socket after all filler drained and the peer returned a marker. The send buffer was 8 or 64 KiB, and the pending suffix was 4 or 32 KiB. Four counterbalanced pairs per cell, 32 new connections overall. Every post-drain observation showed an empty sender queue and matched the default filter's available space. In the 8 KiB buffer / 32 KiB suffix cell, all four candidate sockets still reported writable with only 8 KiB available. Earlier snapshots at registration already showed readiness in 18/32 cases as loopback acknowledgments progressed. Every arm verified 294,912 suffix bytes and both EOFs. The companion measured post-drain readiness and socket capacity. It did not measure intermediate TCP wakeup counts. No timed throughput, packet loss, fresh p95/p99, runnable time or CPU/GiB was scored.

Keep the ordinary writable filter. This request saved no flush writes on the anonymous path and did not guarantee that a real TCP socket had room for the entire suffix. Revisit a write-coalescing policy only after the target profile shows repeated partial flushes and binds the effective credit to actual byte progress.
