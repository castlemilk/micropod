### 24 November 2026 · 233.02 · Where an ipvlan L3 service reply resolves its next hop

The stock private macvlan driver handed the TCP reply and the pod's gateway ARP request directly to the lower transmit queue (2/2). With ipvlan L3, the same TCP reply requested an IPv4 route in the parent namespace, then reached the parent local-output callback (1/1). Its ARP request reached the driver's drop branch (1/1). **The L3 candidate needs a parent-namespace route and neighbor qualification. Reusing the stock pod neighbor-refresh check would miss that requirement.**
