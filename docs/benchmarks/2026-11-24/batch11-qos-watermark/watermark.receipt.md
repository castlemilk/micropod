### 24 November 2026 · 230.06 · A 128 KiB read watermark on open connections during sustained bulk

**Keep ordinary read readiness.** Darwin host-only, real IPv4 loopback TCP. Receiver-measured bulk was **4,425.4 → 4,487.6 MiB/s** (ordinary → adaptive arm medians). The median paired change was **+0.77%** (95% whole-pair percentile bootstrap −1.51% to +3.63%, 20,000 resamples). The adaptive watermark won 4/8 pairs. Loaded open-reply p95/p99 rose from **0.189/0.236 ms to 2.556/2.573 ms**, pooled over 96 open replies per arm. Every pair had a higher candidate p95.

**Control and workload.** One binary ran the same archived dedicated data-owner relay in both arms. Only the candidate enabled the original adaptive policy after 256 KiB of receiver history, promoting the read threshold to 128 KiB and arming its nominal 2 ms timer. A serial dispatch read-source used the production `.userInitiated` setting. All observed callbacks reported QoS class 25 on both arms. Both arms used one continuously supplied bulk flow and 12 preconnected short-reply flows. Each short flow delivered its 256 KiB seed before sending a verified 17-byte reply, and stayed open. This measures short replies on established connections. Fresh-connect latency and packet-level drops were unmeasured.

**First attempts and guardrails.** Eight shuffled paired blocks (seed 23006, four in each order), no retries. All 16 attempts delivered byte-identical bulk data and all 192 markers arrived inside their fixed 900 ms receiver windows. Including pre-window and drained bulk work, verified sent/received totals were 35,317,022,720 bytes (ordinary) and 35,253,125,120 bytes (adaptive). All 96 adaptive markers were sent with demotion pending. The timer was serviced a median **2.388 ms** after registration (p95 **2.536 ms**, max **2.552 ms**). These are handler service times; kernel expiry and callback delivery were not separated. The pooled p99 is the highest of 96 markers. Median CPU cost was 0.444 → 0.433 CPU-s/GiB and runnable time was 2.444 → 2.446 thread-s/s across the whole fixture, including producer and verifier. The largest CPU endpoint offset from the intended sample boundary was 5.03 ms.

**Scope and next check.** Both TCP legs used ephemeral 127.0.0.1 endpoints on the same M5 Max. The host was shared: 45 ambient samples recorded 23.9–71.0% interval CPU idle (median 54.4%) and at least 368.1 GiB available on the project volume. This diagnostic provides no VM, independent peer, or new-connection throughput verdict. The short-reply regression and uncertain rate change give no basis for adopting this policy.
